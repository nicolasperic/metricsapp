# Registration

---

- [Registration](#registration)

Access the <a href="{{ url('register') }}" target="_blank">registration page</a> and fill in the required fields:
- **Name**
- **E-Mail Address**: valid email address *(no email validation required at the moment)*
- **Password**: password must be at least 8 characters
- **Password Confirmation**: must match password


As soon as you finish registration you will be logged in to the app and redirected to the [Settings page](/{{route}}/{{version}}/settings). You will be asked to enter your `Assembla Key` and `Secret` to begin using the app.


> {info} Key and Secret can be found in [Assembla](https://app.assembla.com/user/edit/manage_clients).
Be sure you check `API Access` when generating a new key to allow API authentication.
